# 107. What are the trade-offs of CopyOnWrite collections?

> **Section:** 6. Concurrent Collections · **Level:** Practitioner to architect

## TL;DR

Read simplicity comes from making each mutation more costly.

## How it works

Readers are cheap and iteration is safe, but every mutation copies the array, concurrent writers serialize, and iterators retain old arrays until released. This increases allocation and garbage-collection pressure. Evaluate write frequency, collection size, and how stale a reader may be.

## Architect insight

Estimate write cost as collection size times mutation frequency; measure GC as well as throughput.

## Common pitfall

Do not pick it just to avoid reader locks when writes or freshness matter.
