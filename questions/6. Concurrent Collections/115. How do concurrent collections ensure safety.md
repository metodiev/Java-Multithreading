# 115. How do concurrent collections ensure thread safety?

> **Section:** 6. Concurrent Collections · **Level:** Practitioner to architect

## TL;DR

Concurrent collections use structure-specific synchronization, CAS, publication, snapshots, or lock-free algorithms.

## How it works

They combine synchronization, CAS, volatile state, immutable snapshots, or lock-free algorithms according to their design. Their public contracts specify allowed concurrent observations. Thread safety is operation-specific: composing individually safe calls does not automatically make the sequence atomic.

## Example

```java
ConcurrentMap<String, Config> configs = new ConcurrentHashMap<>();
```

## Architect insight

Reason from the public contract and protect cross-entry invariants separately.

## Common pitfall

Thread-safe methods do not make a compound sequence atomic.
