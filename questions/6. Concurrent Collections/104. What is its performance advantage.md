# 104. What is the performance advantage of ConcurrentHashMap?

> **Section:** 6. Concurrent Collections · **Level:** Practitioner to architect

## TL;DR

Independent keys can often progress without serializing on one map-wide lock.

## How it works

Independent keys can often be read or updated concurrently without a global map lock, yielding better throughput than synchronizing every operation on one wrapper. Hot keys and hash collisions still contend. Map-wide operations and aggregate observations may not be atomic snapshots during concurrent updates.

## Example

```java
map.putIfAbsent(requestId, result);
```

## Architect insight

Benchmark realistic key skew and write ratios; concurrency does not imply linear speedup.

## Common pitfall

Aggregate observations and traversals are not atomic snapshots during updates.
