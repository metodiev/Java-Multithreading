# 139. What are the benefits of immutability in multi-threaded design?

> **Section:** 7. Concurrency Patterns · **Level:** Practitioner to architect

## TL;DR

Immutability simplifies safe sharing, caching, retry, and concurrent reads.

## How it works

Immutable objects eliminate races on their fields after construction, support safe sharing, and simplify caching and retries. They can make updates allocation-heavy; persistent data structures or atomic replacement of whole snapshots can help. Ensure construction is safe and do not expose mutable internals.

## Example

```java
AtomicReference<Config> config = new AtomicReference<>(initialConfig);
```

## Architect insight

Publish replacement snapshots atomically when readers need a coherent configuration.

## Common pitfall

Final references do not freeze mutable child objects.
